# ADR-0008: Emergency pause

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

Duplication, insolvency, malicious mods, or bridge compromise may require immediate containment. Ordinary market volatility must remain outside emergency discretion.

## Decision

The Verse DAO Safe or delegated Security Council may pause narrowly scoped economic or content functions. A pause has no automatic expiration and remains until authorized unpause.

## Consequences

- Incidents can be contained for as long as remediation requires.
- Scope and rationale must be public.
- Governance-key security is critical.
- The pause system must not provide discretionary price control or confiscation.
