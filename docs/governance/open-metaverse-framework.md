# Open Metaverse governing framework

**Status:** Accepted product-governance baseline

## Canonical selection

The Verse adopts the “Open Metaverse” as its contractual governing framework using the meaning defined in [Section 13 of the Bittrees Bounties Terms of Use](https://bounties.bittrees.org/terms), effective 2026-08-12. The founder confirmed this selection and its legal preview on 2026-08-26.

The referenced framework centers on:

- Self-sovereignty and informed consent.
- Respect for property and voluntarily accepted contracts.
- Transparent rules and code-backed evidence.
- Interoperability and decentralization.
- Portable reputation based on experience.
- Nonaggression against people, property, systems, and lawful autonomy.
- Authority and enforcement no broader than code or voluntary agreement actually grants.

## Mandatory-rights boundary

The Open Metaverse is a contractual framework and set of principles. The Verse shall not describe it as a nation, sovereign, court, territorial jurisdiction, or immunity from law.

Nothing in the framework waives nonwaivable consumer protection, privacy, remedy, forum, or other mandatory rights. Applicable nonwaivable law prevails to the extent of a conflict. A provision that cannot be enforced is limited only as much as necessary while the remainder continues.

## Product and protocol consequences

The Verse shall:

- Present material wallet signatures, token approvals, deadlines, custody changes, market rules, and deterministic outcomes before consent.
- Favor verifiable performance and direct resolution between participants.
- Keep protocol authority explicit, narrow, inspectable, and attributable.
- Publish the rules, code versions, content manifests, and governance actions that affect participant rights or assets.
- Preserve ordinary market autonomy while allowing only the narrow integrity and security interventions defined by accepted governance requirements.
- Offer portable public records and proofs without exposing private data unnecessarily.

## Versioning and release control

Each public deployment shall identify the exact terms version and effective date accepted by its users. The release record must preserve a durable copy or content hash so a later website edit cannot silently rewrite an existing agreement.

Material framework changes require a public proposal, legal and security review appropriate to the change, Verse DAO approval, a new effective date, and prospective user consent where required. An update does not retroactively rewrite completed transactions or previously accepted contracts.

Before a real-value public launch, the release owner must verify that the hosted terms, privacy and acceptable-use notices, operator disclosures, consent surfaces, and deployment-specific mandatory obligations implement this approved framework.
