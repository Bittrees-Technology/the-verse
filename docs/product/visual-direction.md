# Visual direction

**Status:** Proposed

## Working identity

**Name:** The Verse

The desired mood combines:

- Utilitarian industrial space hardware.
- Monumental and mysterious transit or portal infrastructure.
- Expeditionary military organization.
- Harsh frontier survival.
- Lived-in interiors and visible maintenance.
- The isolation and scale of deep space.

The Verse will not reproduce protected creatures, ships, uniforms, architecture, symbols, interfaces, sounds, names, or story elements from Alien, Stargate, Starship Troopers, Space Engineers, or any other franchise.

## Original visual pillars

### Industrial truth

Equipment communicates how it works. Power buses, conveyors, access panels, radiators, pressure doors, fasteners, wear, and repair marks are visible.

### Ancient geometry

Rare large-scale structures use an original geometric language suggesting technology older than current civilization. Avoid recognizable ring-gate compositions or copied glyph systems.

### Expeditionary civilization

Stations and vessels express logistics, hierarchy, preparedness, and adaptation rather than glossy utopia. Civilian, corporate, scientific, and defense organizations share a modular manufacturing heritage.

### Dangerous emptiness

Space is not filled with constant spectacle. Long darkness, faint stars, distant industrial light, dust, and radio traffic communicate scale.

### Human-scale readability

Even enormous structures must retain recognizable doors, ladders, cargo routes, service spaces, warning systems, and maintenance access.

### Celestial scale truth

Planets, moons, and asteroids communicate scale through consistent one-metre
construction units, canonical body radius and range, stable angular size,
atmospheric limb, cloud altitude, terrain frequency, light direction, and
deliberate empty space. A proof-scale body is labelled as proof-scale and is not
presented as production geography. A local asteroid cannot look attached to a
distant planet merely because of camera composition.

A body beyond the local render range may use a camera-relative proxy only when
its direction and angular diameter remain consistent with its canonical fixed
address and radius. Near/far tiers overlap or cross-fade without a visible pop,
shift, or scale jump. Stars are an effectively infinite camera-centered sky and
do not translate like dust a few hundred metres away. Local particulate matter
uses restrained size, motion, and illumination so it cannot be mistaken for a
star, moon, asteroid, target, or ore signal.

### Streaming continuity

Entering replication range should feel like receiving detail about a stable
world, not spawning a new one. Unchanged pilots, grids, and voxel chunks retain
stable visual identity across updates. `OUT OF LOCAL VIEW`, destruction, and
cross-cell transfer use distinct original transitions and language. Re-entry
cannot create a duplicate, replay an obsolete destruction effect, or restore an
actionable stale marker.

Loading and failure states are part of the visual system. Native and browser
clients distinguish connecting, registry loading, cell loading, live,
stream-constrained, stale, reconnecting, and fatal projection states. An open
socket alone does not visually imply live authority. Missing body art uses a
neutral labelled proxy instead of hiding an authoritative collider or gravity
source.

### Maps and instrumentation

The native HUD uses a compact original hierarchy for gravity source, nearest
body, range, altitude, atmosphere, breathability, connection phase, and stream
health. Information supports physical work without becoming a copied cockpit
or terminal composition.

The browser separates an exact fixed-body universe map from a fitted local-cell
observer map. Hierarchical or logarithmic navigation, distance scales, pan,
zoom, direct marker selection, last-update age, and explicit view bounds make
large coordinate differences readable. Map shapes, icons, colors, typography,
panels, terminology, animations, and sounds remain Verse designs; functional
similarity to a genre interface is not permission to reproduce its expression.

## Initial palette

The exact palette remains an art-direction task. The prototype should use neutral original materials:

- Charcoal structural composites.
- Warm off-white pressure interiors.
- Oxidized metals.
- Restrained amber work lighting.
- Cyan or green instrumentation used sparingly.
- Organization colors applied as replaceable panels and markings.

## Clean-room review

Every production concept must retain source references and pass an originality
review. “Inspired by” is not approval to reproduce another franchise's
distinctive expression. General observable mechanics such as fixed planets,
voxel mining, six-axis EVA, engineering terminals, spatial streaming, and
angularly correct distant bodies must be independently specified and
implemented. Review explicitly rejects copied layouts, measurements, icon
silhouettes, color systems, shader compositions, names, text, sounds, code,
assets, and recognizable presentation sequences.
