# Product vision

**Status:** Accepted

The Verse is a persistent industrial space universe where human players, bots, NPCs, and AI agents discover resources, build infrastructure, organize companies, perform work, fight, trade, and leave an auditable economic history.

## Player promise

A player can begin with nothing, find resources, produce useful goods, join or form an organization, build at increasingly large scales, and participate in markets priced primarily in BIT.

The universe continues when a player logs out. Travel, power usage, production, defense, attack, decay, markets, and contracts remain meaningful persistent processes.

The Verse is differentiated by a persistent public industrial society, not by
the number of blocks it exposes or by reproducing another product. A useful
object carries material origin, labor, ownership, location, operating history,
and consequences that remain meaningful to other participants.

## Product pillars

### Physical industry

Economic value begins with physical location and transformation. Ore must be found, mined, hauled, refined, manufactured, installed, defended, and sometimes destroyed.

### Persistent consequence

Structures and ships remain in the universe. Offline assets may be attacked. Carried inventory drops at death. Unpowered objects become derelict and are removed according to public rules.

### Vast frontier

The generated universe has no reachable practical boundary. Planets remain fixed and widely separated. Asteroids may form dense groups, while new frontier sectors provide continued resource expansion.

### Open economy

BIT is the primary base pair. The capital market is the default venue, but every qualified location or organization may operate a market. Prices are algorithmic and location-specific.

### Open participation

Humans, bots, NPCs, and AI agents may play and use published interfaces under the same authoritative rules. Anonymous viewing is supported; playing and transaction signing use passkey accounts.

### Open development

The official client and server are open source. SDKs and schemas are permissively licensed. Official content changes use public specifications and governance.

## Experience principles

- **Feel before breadth:** movement, camera behavior, targeting, mining,
  welding, cutting, inventory, and construction must be trustworthy before the
  project expands the block catalog.
- **Work creates progress:** extraction, hauling, manufacturing, construction,
  repair, survey, salvage, security, and logistics are first-class careers and
  the primary sources of reputation and progression.
- **One construction grammar:** the same interoperable functional-block model
  supports bases, ships, factories, vehicles, and infrastructure.
- **A remembered world:** the universe records what participants discover,
  build, mine, damage, repair, trade, abandon, and recover.
- **One authority model:** humans and autonomous participants use the same
  server-authoritative, conservation-preserving rules through native, browser,
  and published interfaces.

## Explicit non-goals for the first implementation

- A single server process simulating the entire universe.
- Full browser parity with the native physics client.
- Running gameplay state directly in smart contracts.
- Importing private-server items into the official economy.
- Copying another game's code, assets, UI, fiction, or signature designs.
- Guaranteeing infinite BIT liquidity at a fixed commodity price.
