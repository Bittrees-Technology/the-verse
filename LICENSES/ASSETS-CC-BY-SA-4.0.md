# Reusable asset license

Unless an individual file states otherwise, reusable original art assets designated as community assets are intended to be released under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license.

Canonical legal text:

- [CC BY-SA 4.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode)
- SPDX identifier: `CC-BY-SA-4.0`

Every asset submission must include:

- Creator or rights holder.
- Source file.
- Creation date.
- License.
- Required attribution.
- Any source references.
- Confirmation that it does not copy protected third-party material.

Official logos, wordmarks, and identity assets are not automatically covered by this asset license.
