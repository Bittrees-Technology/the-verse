# Community code of conduct

The Verse community is committed to open, rigorous, and respectful collaboration.

Contributors must:

- Treat people with respect regardless of identity, experience, role, location, or viewpoint.
- Critique ideas and changes without attacking people.
- Disclose conflicts of interest.
- Respect security embargoes and private user information.
- Credit creative and technical work.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, impersonation, and deliberate disruption.
- Avoid using governance, market access, or technical privilege for retaliation.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or refer serious matters to the appropriate platform or authority when needed to protect participants and the project.

A formal governance-approved enforcement and appeal process must be adopted before the community opens broadly.
